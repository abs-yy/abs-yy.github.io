The anhydrobiotic insect cell line Pv11, established from Polypedilum vanderplanki embryos, enables molecular studies of extreme desiccation tolerance. This study presents a chemical transfection method using FuGENE® HD as an alternative to electroporation. While requiring longer incubation, this approach markedly reduces cytotoxicity while maintaining robust gene delivery, providing a more practical method for genetic manipulation of Pv11 cells.

Recommended citation: Yoshida Y., Pratama G. A., Hotta M., and Kikawada T. (2025) Effective chemical transfection for an anhydrobiotic insect cell line. Cryobiol Cryotechnol, 71, 2:81-88.
